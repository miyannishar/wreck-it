Meet wreck it. It turns your coding agent into a QA team for your local web app.

Start your app locally, install the skills, and set up the browser tools. Your agent uses them to explore the app and record what breaks.

Ask it to test your app, or focus on one flow, like checkout. The agent walks through the flow, checks edge cases, and reproduces the problems it finds.

Here is a seeded demo bug. Three pairs of earbuds should total two hundred thirty-nine dollars and ninety-seven cents. Checkout displays just seventy-nine ninety-nine.

The finding includes reproduction steps, relevant screenshots, and the source location. Here, the subtotal adds each unit price once, ignoring the quantity.

Open the report and review the generated Playwright test. This check fails on the buggy copy, then passes after the subtotal calculation is corrected.
